About Chemistry Location

Learn Chemistry, One Concept at a Time

Chemistry Location is an educational website for school students of Class 9 to 12. Our goal is simple: make chemistry clear, organised and easy to revise, so that every concept “clicks into place”.

What you will find here

  • Class-wise study material for Class 9, 10, 11 and 12, arranged chapter by chapter.
  • Notes and short revision sheets written in simple language.
  • Video lectures and links that explain concepts step by step.
  • Questions, PYQs and practice material to test what you have learned.
  • An interactive periodic table with all 118 elements, their symbols, atomic numbers, groups and periods.

Who runs this website

Chemistry Location is created and maintained by Zeeshan. The website is designed to work well on mobile phones, because most students study on their phones.

How we keep content useful

  • We organise material around common school syllabi and textbooks such as NCERT, and we aim to keep it simple and accurate.
  • New notes and resources are added regularly from our owner-only admin panel.
  • If you find a mistake, please tell us through the contact page. We review every report and correct errors as soon as we can.

Free to use

You do not need to create an account or log in to read the study material. To keep the website running, we may show advertisements in the future. Please read our Privacy Policy to see how we handle your information, and our Disclaimer to understand how to use the material.
