Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Chemistry Location (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. You can read all study material without creating an account. If you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the name you type, your email address (optional), the subject and your message. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

Information collected automatically. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, the hosting provider and other infrastructure services may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you request. We do not currently run our own analytics tool. If we add one, we will update this page.

2. How we use information

  • To read and respond to your messages, and to correct mistakes you report.
  • To keep the website secure and working, and to prevent spam or misuse.
  • To improve our study material and the website.
  • To show advertising, if and when advertising is enabled (see section 5).

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The public study pages do not require you to log in and do not use cookies to track you. The website owner’s private admin panel stores a login session in the owner’s own browser. If advertising is enabled, advertising partners such as Google may place or read cookies in your browser as described in section 5.

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some third-party services work.

4. Third-party services we use

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website files.
  • Supabase — stores our chapters, resources, uploaded files and contact-form messages.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the fonts used on the site. When fonts load, Google may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • jsDelivr / unpkg — content delivery networks that deliver a code library used by the website.
  • YouTube and other external sites — if you open a video or external link we share, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may show ads provided by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this website and other websites on the internet. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google’s advertising policies.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before ads that use cookies or personal data are shown.

6. How long we keep information

Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and to keep a record of reported errors. You can ask us to delete your message at any time using the contact page.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent where we rely on it. Send your request through the contact page, and choose “Privacy request” as the subject.

8. Children’s privacy

Our study material is meant for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you send us any personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, database access rules that allow visitors to read only published study material, and an owner-only login for managing content. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use our contact page.
